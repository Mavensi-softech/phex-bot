Privacy Policy — PHEX BOT Chrome Extension

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

1. Overview

PHEX BOT is a Chrome extension intended for authorized healthcare personnel. It automates the transfer of patient and prescription information between supported healthcare applications.

The extension is designed to work with the following applications:

  • CareSpan Clinic
  • HealthMed

It does not provide general-purpose web scraping functionality or operate on unrelated websites.

2. Information Accessed

The extension may access information displayed within the supported healthcare applications, including:

  • Patient name
  • Patient identification information
  • Date of birth or age
  • UHID or other patient identifiers
  • Prescription information
  • Prescription number
  • Medication name
  • Dosage
  • Frequency
  • Duration
  • Doctor or prescribing information

Only information required for the extension's intended functionality is processed.

3. Data Processing and Storage

Information accessed by the extension is processed locally on the user's device.

The extension does not maintain a backend server or developer-controlled database for storing patient or prescription information.

Temporary information required during the extension's operation may be stored using Chrome's chrome.storage.session API and is associated with the current browser session.

4. Data Sharing

Patient and prescription information accessed by the extension is not sold, rented, or provided to third parties for advertising, marketing, or analytics purposes.

The extension does not send this information to the developer or unrelated external services. Information is used only to perform the authorized workflow between the supported healthcare applications.

5. Permissions

The extension uses the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab — to interact with the active supported healthcare application page.
  • scripting — to perform the required extraction and data-entry operations on supported pages.
  • storage — to temporarily maintain information required during the extension's operation.
  • downloads — to support the extension's file-related functionality when applicable.
  • Host permissions — to restrict extension functionality to the supported healthcare application domains.

6. Data Security

The extension is designed to process healthcare information locally and does not maintain a remote database containing the information accessed through the extension.

Users should use the extension only on authorized devices and accounts and follow their organization's applicable security and data-protection requirements.

7. Intended Users

PHEX BOT is intended for authorized healthcare personnel who have legitimate access to the supported healthcare applications.

The extension must be used only for authorized purposes and in accordance with applicable organizational policies and laws.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the extension or applicable requirements. The Last Updated date will be revised whenever the policy is changed.
